Two primary frameworks govern departure holdroom sizing in North American and international practice. ACRP prescribes deterministic component-level targets; IATA establishes performance ranges based on operational variables.

Planning Framework Reference

ACRP — Component-Driven Architectural Prescription

Standard North American practice aligned with FAA-funded research. Sizing is deterministic based on physical aircraft capacity and fixed spatial constraints (e.g., 15 ft queue depth, 8 ft corridor) to ensure high predictability across planning scenarios.

IATA — Performance-Driven Operational Ranges

Global ADRM framework establishing lower/upper boundaries for an "Optimum" Level of Service. Accounts for operational variables like carrier business models (LCC vs. Legacy), check-in technology, and concession-retaining "Call-to-Gate" strategies.
